ABOUT COME SIT BESIDE ME

You've come to visit her.

She lives with dementia. Her memories drift, change and fade. Move your light slowly to find them.

This is an artistic interpretation, not a representation of every experience of dementia. The memories are fictional. If you use the camera, it stays on your device and nothing is recorded.

Explore with your pointer, touch, or arrow keys. Tab reveals the controls. The Controls button also opens them. Space pauses drifting; Esc closes this window. Sound is off by default.
